FAQ

In brief

What does Polyhedra do?

Polyhedra is an independent PR and strategic content boutique based in Milan, founded by Sara Noggler in 2018. It specialises in innovation communications: press office and media relations, thought leadership for founders and executives, editorial content and institutional events.

Which sectors do you work in?

Fintech and digital finance, crypto and blockchain, artificial intelligence and technology, wellness and longevity, policy and public institutions. Projects include Gemini, Wirex, Sandwich Club, Rebliss and Future Blend's Rome Policy Forum.

Do you work with international companies?

Yes. We support international companies entering the Italian market, such as Gemini and Wirex, building their presence in national and trade media and their relationships with Italian institutions.

What is LinkedIn thought leadership?

It is the work that turns founders and executives into recognised voices in their industry. We define positioning and editorial line, write the content together with the person, and measure its effect on reputation and opportunities.

What is Future Blend?

Future Blend is the institutional digital finance platform founded by Polyhedra. On 4 February 2026 it held the Rome Policy Forum in the Sala della Lupa of the Italian Chamber of Deputies, with PwC Italy and Wirex, under the patronage of the European Parliament, AssoFintech and AssoCASP.
