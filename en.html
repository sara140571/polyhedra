Questions

In short

What does Polyhedra do?

Polyhedra organises institutional events and runs press office, public affairs and LinkedIn programmes for companies and leaders who bring innovation, from finance to technology, from health to energy. It is based in Milan, at Via Emilio Visconti Venosta 3.

What kind of events do you organise?

Forums, institutional dinners, round tables and study launches, in Milan and Rome. We handle topics, speakers, invitations, venues and media coverage.

Who is behind Polyhedra?

Sara Noggler, who founded it in 2018, with a team of ten professionals across media relations, content, events and public affairs.

Do you organise institutional events in Rome too?

Yes. We organise forums and conferences in Milan and Rome, including in institutional venues, with speakers from institutions, business and finance.

Do you run press office for companies?

Yes. We manage press office and journalist relations with Italian national and trade media, for Italian and international companies.

Do you work with international companies?

Yes. We have supported companies such as Gemini and Wirex in Italy with press office, events and public affairs.

About

Sara Noggler

She founded Polyhedra in 2018 after years in financial communications. She is a LinkedIn Top Voice on digital finance, vice president of the Sandwich Club think tank and writes the NEXT column for the Italian news agency Prima Pagina News.

She works with ten professionals across media relations, content, events and public affairs.
